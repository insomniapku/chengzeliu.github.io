About

Student, builder, learner.

A personal homepage for research notes and work in progress.

I am currently studying in the School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science at Peking University. My academic interest centers on robotics: how robots perceive their environment, learn from data, and eventually complete useful physical tasks.

This site keeps my public profile simple: research direction, contact information, projects when they are ready, and notes from my personal blog.

Research
